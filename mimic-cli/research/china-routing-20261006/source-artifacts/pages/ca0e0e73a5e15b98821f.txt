About Us
AiPrice (formerly AliPrice) is a company specializing in the development and operation of SaaS software for cross-border e-commerce.
In July 2013, the studio was established.
In December 2014, Dongguan Chengpai Network Technology Co., Ltd. (东莞市橙派网络科技有限公司)was founded.
In 2018, it was awarded the title of Dongguan High-tech Enterprise.
In November 2023, it won the honor of being among the first batch of partners of 1688 cross-border e-commerce.
In December 2023 AiPrice became the Official Cross-border Service Provider of 1688.
In August 2025, AiPrice was awarded as the 2025 New Power Partner by WorldFirst.
In February 2026, the AliPrice brand was officially upgraded to AiPrice.
As of August 2025, AiPrice has 1 million total users, with nearly 650,000 registered.Its website ranks around 20,000 globally on Alexa, and around 1,000 in South Korea.Over 70% of Korean merchants use the AiPrice plugin, demonstrating its influence and popularity in the international market.
