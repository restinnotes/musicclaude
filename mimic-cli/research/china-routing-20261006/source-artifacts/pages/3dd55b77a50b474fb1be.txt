CONNECTED COMMERCE WORKFLOW
How DSers integrations connect your commerce workflow
DSers integrations connect the services used before, during, and after an order. Link supported supplier platforms and sales channels, process connected-store orders in DSers, and return fulfillment updates through the supported workflow.
Workflow stageWithout a connected workflowWith DSers integrations
Source productsSupplier research and store data live in separate tools.Use supported sourcing platforms and map products to the stores connected to DSers.
Process ordersOrder details are checked and transferred between systems.Bring supported store orders into DSers, select the mapped supplier and shipping method, and place the supplier order.
Update fulfillmentTracking numbers are copied back manually.When tracking becomes available, DSers can return the update through the supported store workflow and, where applicable, PayPal.

CHOOSE YOUR CONNECTION PATH
Bring each part of your commerce workflow into DSers
DSers partners serve different roles, so there is no single setup path for every integration. Start with the connection type that matches the part of your workflow you want to manage.
01
Sales channels
Connect supported storefronts and marketplaces from DSers through LINK TO MORE STORES, then follow the platform-specific authorization flow. Confirm connected stores in the Store Switcher or under Settings > Application Management > Sales Channel.
02
Sourcing partners
Choose a supported supplier platform or the DSers Verified Agent Network to build the sourcing route that fits your products and fulfillment needs. Available connection, ordering, and payment steps vary by partner.
03
Payment and tracking services
Use PayPal or Stripe for the eligible payment workflows supported by each service. Where applicable, DSers can also return supplier tracking updates through the connected workflow.
04
CSV and custom storefronts
If your platform or self-built store does not have a direct DSers integration, use the CSV workflow to move supported product or order data into DSers with the current file template.

FAQ
What is DSers E-Commerce Integration?
DSers integrations connect supported sourcing platforms, ecommerce sales channels, and payment methods with the product, order, and tracking workflows available in DSers.Which selling platforms does DSers integrate with?
DSers supports Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce, eBay, TikTok Shop, Amazon, Walmart, Big Cartel, Jumpseller, Cartpanda, Square, Ecwid, BigCommerce, Squarespace, SHOPLINE, LPQV, Magento, Genstore.ai, and CSV workflows. Check the channel list inside DSers for the latest availability.What sourcing platforms does DSers connect to?
DSers supports sourcing workflows for AliExpress, Alibaba.com, 1688, and the DSers Verified Agent Network. Alibaba.com and 1688 include supported routes for dropshipping and wholesale sourcing; connection and payment steps vary by route.What is the difference between 1688 dropshipping and 1688 wholesale in DSers?
1688 dropshipping allows one-piece order fulfillment with inventory, orders, and tracking synced automatically — distinct from 1688 wholesale, which is for bulk procurement at lower unit costs with tiered volume discounts, flexible air or sea shipping, blind ordering, and professional warehousing support.How does DSers handle payment and tracking sync?
DSers supports PayPal and Stripe for eligible subscription and supplier-order payments. When supplier tracking becomes available, DSers can return the update through supported store workflows and, where applicable, PayPal.Can I use DSers if my selling platform is not on the integration list?
CSV import provides a file-based workflow for platforms without a direct DSers connection. Download the current template, complete the required product or order fields, and upload the file to DSers.
