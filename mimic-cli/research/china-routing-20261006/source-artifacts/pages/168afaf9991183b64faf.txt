CTA U.S. Consumer Technology One-Year Industry Forecast, 2022-2027 (July 2026)
The CTA U.S. Consumer Technology One-Year Industry Forecast serves as the benchmark for the consumer technology industry, charting U.S. market size and shipment volumes for over 200 products and services. The one-year industry forecast is grounded in CTA’s unique position in the industry, drawing on the perspective of its members, its dedicated team of research analysts, and its deep knowledge of technology adoption. This semiannual publication provides aggregated data from the more detailed CTA U.S. Consumer Technology Five-Year Industry Forecast.
Inside the report:
Annual data for each product (five-year historical; one-year forecast)
Shipment volumes
Wholesale revenues
Average wholesale prices
Household penetration rate
