User (2026-10-21), after the lines were rewritten in plain, concrete language:

- Prose (散文体) is "anti-psychological-mechanism". Glue sentences like "我知道这些事可能不是一回事……后来我慢慢发现" carry no content.
- The final presentation will not be prose, and prose is tiring to read.
- A bullet / ChatGPT-answer form is likely better. The user approved the step-list bullet version ("读起来确实是比之前要好很多了，这个成果我是认可的").
- Still wanted:
  - more condensed and deeper;
  - a reader sees at a glance that it has real content, without having to extract or "mechanize" it themselves;
  - psychological mechanism ≠ philosophy: never go back to hard-to-read abstraction.
- Beads have the same prose problem ("bead其实也是这样的").
- Also disliked: abstract stance words (站稳、安顿、归属…), and equal-length segments when a segment has little content (1–3 bars getting a full paragraph).

**Why:** the line's value is the causal and psychological mechanism. Prose dilutes it, and the reader has to dig it out.

**How to apply:**
- Line output: per segment 1–3 points: what is happening to him now, why (which earlier segment caused it), the mechanism named in plain words, and where it pushes him next.
- Segment length follows the content.
- No transitions or ornament; no abstract jargon.
