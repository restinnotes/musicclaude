Overview
Buy from China while browsing global shopping sites. One click to find similar items from Chinese platforms and save money!
Buy from China is a plugin that helps you quickly find the same or similar products in China when you browse products on overseas shopping sites (e.g. Amazon, Etsy, Ebay, Walmart, Temu, AliExpress, Shein, etc.). It allows you to jump to our shopping platform with one click, compare prices and find more cost-effective source goodies.
🔍 You will get:
● One-click to find the same item in China from Amazon, Etsy, Ebay, Walmart, etc.
● Precise image search, support 1688, Taobao and other platforms
● No need for complex operation, automatic identification of product images
● Quickly compare the price, save the cost of customs duties
● Suitable for cross-border sellers, surrogate buyers, and seafood consumers
🌏 Try HandHunt Buy from China now to make global shopping smarter!
