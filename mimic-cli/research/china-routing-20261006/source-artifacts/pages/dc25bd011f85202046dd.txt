BigGo Shopping Assistant Affiliate Program Disclosure
Affiliate Links and Commission Disclosure
To maintain our free price comparison service, BigGo has established affiliate partnerships with various e-commerce platforms. When you complete a purchase through BigGo Shopping Assistant's referral links, we may receive a small commission from our partner merchants. These commissions do not affect your purchase price.
Transparent Link Mechanism
We do not modify cookies or links without users' knowledge
All affiliate referrals are completed through user confirmation (via cashback reward notifications on e-commerce sites) or by clicking products redirected from the BigGo website
You are free to choose whether to use our referral links
Data Collection Statement
BigGo Shopping Assistant only collects necessary browsing data and cookie information for:
Tracking product price changes to provide accurate price comparisons
Confirming your shopping information with partner merchants
Please refer to our Privacy Policy for detailed information.
Transparency Commitment
Affiliate commissions are a source of revenue that supports BigGo's continued operations, allowing us to continue providing you with free and convenient price comparison services. Thank you for your understanding and support.
