Overview
AiPrice Sourcing Tool allows you to easily find the source goods on e-commerce websites.
Main features:
1. Find similar products by image
- A powerful image search tool for e-commerce, supporting 1688, Amazon, AliExpress, Alibaba and other shopping platforms. For more supported platforms, please visit the AiPrice(AliPrice) website: https://www.aiprice.com
- Image search methods: click the AiPrice(AliPrice) icon above a product image; right-click and choose screenshot search; upload a local image to search
- Image zoom: on product listing pages, hover over a product image to view it enlarged
2. Practical toolbar
- Download images: batch download product images and videos from Taobao, Amazon, Pinduoduo and other websites; export image links; translate images; enlarge images in high resolution
- Copy link: copy product links from Alibaba, Taobao and other websites with one click. When copying a long link, the selected product SKU can also be copied, and the SKU will be restored when the link is opened again
- Copy title: copy product titles; search for similar products on Taobao using translated titles
- Shipping cost lookup: check shipping costs for different regions with one click
- Export all store products: export all products from a store to an Excel file
- SKU list viewer and price calculator: view all SKU information for AliExpress products with one click
3. Price history and order history
- View price history for products on AliExpress, Amazon and other platforms for free, helping users identify real and fake discounts. Users can add products to favorites and receive notifications when prices drop
- Trends: view one-year price history and order history for products on Amazon and other platforms
4. Amazon Product Pro
- Display key product data directly on Amazon search results pages, including category ranking, BSR ranking, ASIN, whether the product is sold by Amazon, the number of FBA sellers and more
- Quickly view product history trends and product information, helping sellers evaluate competition, sales performance and product research value faster
- Compare data for multiple products in bulk on the listing page without opening product detail pages, improving Amazon product research, product sourcing and competitor analysis efficiency
5. Product related
- Advanced search: includes similar product comparison, filtering and sorting, data export to Excel and other features, helping users find the products they need more accurately
- Product Info PRO: display additional information on product listing pages, such as shipping cost, order price, minimum order quantity, annual sales, listing date, store opening date and more
6. Review related
- Review analysis: calculate the country distribution of reviewers on AliExpress product detail pages
- Download reviews: download product review content and images from Amazon and AliExpress
7. Currency conversion
- Display prices converted into USD, Korean won and dozens of other foreign currencies on Taobao
- Display RMB prices on Temu, Amazon, Alibaba International and other websites
8. Logistics tracking
- Show package delivery information directly on the Taobao order list page, clear at a glance
9. Copy search suggestions
- Copy dropdown keywords and autocomplete suggestions from search boxes on Amazon and other websites
10. Export shopping cart
- Export products from the Taobao shopping cart to an Excel file
11. Browsing history
- Display your browsing history on Taobao, AliExpress and other websites in the bottom-left toolbar
12. Highlight AD labels
- Highlight AD labels for sponsored products, making it easier to identify advertising products
13. Translation features
- AliWangWang translation: translate conversations with sellers; both source and target languages can be translated for barrier-free communication
- Webpage translation: right-click to translate an entire webpage
- Screenshot translation: right-click to capture a screenshot and translate text in the image
14. Minimize plugin
- Collapse or expand the plugin window in the bottom-left corner
