Wegobuy is dead. The site stopped working in early 2025 and has never come back: as of our last direct check on June 6, 2026, wegobuy.com and www.wegobuy.com simply don’t load, and the DNS records tell you why — the domain now resolves through its CDN to an address literally named offline.specialcdnstatus.com, pointing at a null IP. That’s a content delivery network formally flagging a dead origin server, not a temporary outage.
So this page is not a review. It’s the page we think people searching “wegobuy” in 2026 actually need: what the service was, exactly when and how it disappeared, what that means if you had money or parcels with it, and where to go instead. Do not enter payment details on any site claiming to be Wegobuy, and treat any article reviewing it as a working agent as a warning about that article’s author.
What Wegobuy was
Wegobuy launched in 2019 and, per JadeShip’s August 2019 coverage announcing it, was positioned from day one as a sister brand to Superbuy — created so the cautious main brand could distance itself from branded and replica goods while Wegobuy took that traffic. The community consensus throughout its life was blunt: same warehouse, same backend, looser item policy. For roughly 2019–2022 it was the default “rep-friendly Superbuy” recommendation across the hobby.
It was a genuinely full-stack operation right up to the end. The final archived homepage (December 2024) shows a shopping agent covering all the mainland and Hong Kong platforms, an Expert Sourcing service, a 1688 station, a rebate and dropshipping program, an official forum at bbs.wegobuy.com, and iOS/Android apps. Historically it followed the Superbuy fee pattern — no service fee on mainstream platforms, flat fees on secondhand marketplaces, around 10% for expert sourcing — with a top-up wallet model and the same FX-markup complaints Superbuy gets.
One detail from that final homepage deserves a flag: it claimed Wegobuy was “Established by Tencent Co-founder.” That’s the same marketing lore the Superbuy ecosystem repeats about its founder — who is not among Tencent’s five known co-founders. The shared puffery is actually useful evidence of shared lineage, but the claim itself doesn’t hold up, and we mention it here so you recognize it when SEO blogs repeat it as fact.
By 2023–2024 Wegobuy had already lost most of its mindshare to the newer wave of agents. Then it lost everything else.
The timeline of the disappearance
This is the part worth being precise about, because third-party blogs contradict each other. The primary-source record, checked on June 6, 2026:
December 7, 2024 — the Wayback Machine’s snapshot of wegobuy.com/en/ shows a fully operating storefront. The homepage was posting routine EMS/EUB delay notices and Double-11 top-up promotions. Nothing suggested a company winding down.
February 1, 2025 onward — every archived capture returns 502 Bad Gateway, continuing through April 2026. (One root-domain “200” from May 2025 served unrelated Chinese content — a CDN misroute, not the agent.)
Today — www.wegobuy.com and bbs.wegobuy.com CNAME to offline.specialcdnstatus.com, which resolves to 169.254.254.254, a link-local null address. The infrastructure itself says: offline.
So the service went dark somewhere between mid-December 2024 and late January 2025 — abruptly, mid-operation, with active promotions still on the homepage. Users noticed in real time: “WeGoBuy USA Not Working” threads appeared on its own forum, and outage-checker sites logged down reports from users in the US, France, Canada, the UK, Norway, and Australia.
Why it died is unknown. We found no evidence of a raid, legal action, or seizure — and equally no evidence of an orderly wind-down. There was no announcement on the site, the forum, social channels, or in any press we could locate. A five-year-old, formerly top-tier agent just stopped existing.
If you had an account, balance, or parcels
Here’s the honest answer: we found no official process. No published wind-down, no compensation notice, no instructions for users with wallet balances or items sitting in the warehouse at the January 2025 cutoff. Whether those users were ever made whole is, as of June 2026, undocumented.
You will see claims that Wegobuy “merged into AllChinaBuy.” There was no redirect, no official statement, and no filing supporting that — it appears to be community shorthand for users from the same presumed operator family migrating on their own. Don’t treat an unrelated signup page as the custodian of your old balance.
The durable lesson applies to every agent, including the ones we recommend: never park money in an agent wallet longer than needed, and never let parcels sit in a warehouse longer than your consolidation actually requires. Wegobuy is the cleanest proof in this hobby’s history that a large, established, fully functional agent can vanish overnight with zero notice.
Why “WeGoBuy Review 2026” articles are red flags
Search “wegobuy review” today and you’ll find SEO sites publishing fresh “WeGoBuy Review 2026 — is it worth it?” articles, complete with fee tables and signup walkthroughs, for a service that has been offline for roughly seventeen months. Even JadeShip — the most established aggregator in the space — still carries a Wegobuy agent page and affiliate link with no live data behind it.
This is genuinely useful information, just not about Wegobuy. A site reviewing a dead agent as a live one is telling you its “reviews” are written from templates and other people’s old posts, without anyone ever loading the site they’re recommending. Whatever that site says about agents that are alive deserves the same confidence. If a review doesn’t tell you when it was last verified, assume it wasn’t.
