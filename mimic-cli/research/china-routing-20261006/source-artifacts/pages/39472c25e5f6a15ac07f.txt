2025 Technology Lifecycle: Consumer Ownership & Upgrade Insights
Published on October 2025
Audio and Video
Digital Health
Smart Home
This report explores U.S. consumer attitudes and behaviors surrounding the purchase and lifecycle of new technology products and accessories, covering a range of 26 consumer technology items.
Specifically, this report aims to:
Measure household technology ownership and purchase intent across the most commonly owned consumer technology products
Assess the expected useful life and current age of consumer technology products/accessories
Determine new and used/certified refurbished purchase rates
Identify factors that lead to the end of useful life for products
Gauge the frequency of product upgrades and replacement
