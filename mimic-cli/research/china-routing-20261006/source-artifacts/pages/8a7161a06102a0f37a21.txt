2024 AIES Data
For information on how we protect confidentiality, please visit the Census Bureau's How We Protect Your Information and Data Protection and Privacy Policy.
Data from the AIES are disseminated in the following formats:
Data Tables- Access formatted tables in a default view on data.census.gov.  Users can modify tables; add/remove geographies and NAICS codes; download in xls, csv, and ZIP formats; and view notes.
FTP (File Transfer Protocol) files- Download complete data tables and/or datasets in zip format. All FTP files for the AIES can be found in the FTP Directory. For instructions on how to download FTP files, view the AIES FAQs.
API (Application Programming Interface)- Access complete datasets in html, xml, or json formats. See AIES APIs for details. This is useful for programmers who want to create their own downloads from the complete dataset.
FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)- FRED, a comprehensive online database maintained by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, now incorporates the Census Bureau’s AIES time series data.

Experimental Data Products
Capital Expenditures for the AIES
This product provides capital expenditures estimates to supplement the Annual Integrated Economic Survey (AIES).
Experimental Small Area State Estimates for the AIES
This product provides small area (state) industry estimates of employment, payroll, first quarter payroll and revenue to supplement the Annual Integrated Economic Survey (AIES).
Robotic Equipment for the AIES
This product provides estimates for expenditures on robotics equipment to supplement the Annual Integrated Economic Survey (AIES).
Product (NAPCS) Projections for the Annual Integrated Economic Survey
This product provides product (NAPCS) estimates of revenue to supplement the Annual Integrated Economic Survey (AIES).
