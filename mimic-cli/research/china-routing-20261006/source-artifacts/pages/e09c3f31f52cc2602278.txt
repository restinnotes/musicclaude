U.S. laptop market highlights
The U.S. laptop market generated a revenue of USD 54,784.3 million in 2025 and is expected to reach USD 75,978.3 million by 2033.
The U.S. market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 4.2% from 2026 to 2033.
In terms of segment, traditional laptop was the largest revenue generating type in 2025.
2-in-1 Laptop is the most lucrative type segment registering the fastest growth during the forecast period.
