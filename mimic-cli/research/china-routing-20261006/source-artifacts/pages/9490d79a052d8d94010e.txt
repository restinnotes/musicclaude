Overview
Find Amazon products cheaper on 1688.com. See landed UK costs & profit instantly. Sourcing, QC & UK delivery by IK Global Sourcing.
🏭 SOURCE SMARTER. PROFIT MORE. SHIP WITH CONFIDENCE.
IK Sourcing — 1688 Profit Scanner is a free Chrome extension that helps Amazon sellers, importers and online retailers instantly find cheaper Chinese suppliers for any product they're selling or researching.
Browse Amazon UK or Amazon US, click the extension icon, and within seconds you'll see matching wholesale suppliers from 1688.com (China's largest B2B marketplace) — complete with estimated landed UK costs and profit margin calculations.
No more guessing. No more manual searching. Just clear numbers to help you make faster, smarter sourcing decisions.
──────────────────────────────
🔍 HOW IT WORKS
──────────────────────────────
1. Navigate to any Amazon product listing
2. Click the IK Sourcing extension icon in your Chrome toolbar
3. The extension automatically detects the product using title and image recognition
4. It searches 1688.com for matching wholesale suppliers in seconds
5. You see landed UK cost estimates and estimated profit margin
6. View the product directly on 1688, or tap one button to WhatsApp our logistics team for a full shipment quote
You can also type any keyword manually to search 1688 directly — no need to be on an Amazon page.
──────────────────────────────
💷 WHAT YOU SEE FOR EVERY RESULT
──────────────────────────────
✅ Product image from the 1688 supplier listing
✅ Estimated landed UK cost (CNY converted to GBP, inc. shipping estimate + VAT)
✅ Estimated profit vs. the live Amazon selling price
✅ Direct link to the supplier listing on 1688.com
✅ One-tap WhatsApp button to request a full shipment quote from IK Sourcing
──────────────────────────────
🚢 WHO IS THIS FOR?
──────────────────────────────
→ Amazon FBA & FBM sellers looking to cut sourcing costs
→ eBay and Etsy sellers sourcing wholesale stock
→ Independent importers bringing goods from China to the UK
→ Retail arbitrage and online arbitrage buyers
→ Dropshippers validating product margins before committing
→ Anyone tired of paying Alibaba prices when 1688 is cheaper
1688.com is the Chinese-language domestic wholesale platform owned by Alibaba Group. Prices are significantly lower than Alibaba or AliExpress because it's designed for Chinese domestic trade — but navigating it without a sourcing agent can be complex. That's where we come in.

🔒 PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS
──────────────────────────────
This extension only requests the permissions it needs:
• activeTab — to read the Amazon product page you're currently viewing
• storage — to temporarily save the product title, image and price while the popup is open
• scripting — to run the product-detection script on Amazon pages
We do not collect personal data. We do not track your browsing history. We do not sell or share any information. Product data is used only to perform the 1688 search and is cleared when you close the extension.
