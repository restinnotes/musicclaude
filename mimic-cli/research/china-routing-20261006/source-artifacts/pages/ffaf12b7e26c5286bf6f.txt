2、Will the goods be delivered to me once I have paid for them? Why do I not need to fill in a shipping address when I make a purchase?
Not all the products on Handhunt.ai are in stock. Our website is an agent website that helps you purchase in China. Therefore, we need to first purchase the goods to our warehouse and help you inspect the goods. After confirming that there are no errors, we will then ask you to fill in the shipping address.
You will need to pay twice during the process from placing your order to receiving the goods:
1. Your first payment is for the cost of purchasing the goods and transporting them to our warehouse.
2. Your second payment is for the cost of international logistics. This includes the logistics costs incurred after we have assisted you in confirming that the quality of the goods is satisfactory, and the goods are sent from our warehouse to your designated address, as well as any customs duties that may be applicable
3、Why is the international shipping cost so expensive in the end, exceeding my expectations, even though the goods I bought are cheap?
International logistics fees are calculated based on the weight and size of the goods. Apart from customs duties, the fees are calculated independently of the value of the goods. Therefore, we recommend that you choose goods with a smaller size and weight for air parcels to avoid high shipping costs.
Due to the large number of categories on our site, the product dimensions and weight automatically filled in by the website are for reference only and are not used as the basis for calculating the final international parcel weight. If you need to obtain detailed weight and size information for the goods, please contact our customer service for a free consultation.
If you need to send a large-sized (longer side over 45 cm) or heavy (more than 12 kg) package, we also provide shipping, rail and land transport, and the destination can be Amazon's warehouse or a private address. The price for shipping via these methods is much lower than air freight, but the delivery time will be longer, usually around 30-35 days. The cost calculation for non-air freight is more complicated, so please contact our customer service for advice.
4、Are the sellers on Handhunt.ai legit? How can I trust you?
We are an agent platform that helps you make purchases in China. Currently, the two platforms we support, Taobao and 1688, both conduct reviews of the authenticity of sellers, and we also review various merchant ratings before you place an order, so you don't need to worry about the merchant being an illegal merchant.
We also verify the quality of the goods. If there are problems with the quality or the merchant fails to deliver, we will help you get a refund as a priority to ensure the safety of your funds from multiple aspects.
However, you should be aware that due to the particularity of international trade, goods that can be legally sold in China do not mean that they can pass through the customs of China or your shipping destination. Please make your own judgment before placing an order. Handhunt.ai does not assume any responsibility for goods that cannot be delivered or returned due to legal reasons such as seizure by customs.
