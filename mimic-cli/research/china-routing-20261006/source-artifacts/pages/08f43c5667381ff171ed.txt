FAQ
What is the DSers Chrome Extension?
The DSers Chrome Extension is a free browser tool that sits in your Chrome toolbar, transforming AliExpress — and beyond — into a seamless sourcing channel. Powered by DSers, it brings automation directly into your daily workflow, eliminating copy-pasting and manual data entry.What can I do with the DSers Chrome Extension?
The extension supports three core functions: Multi-platform Price Comparison via screenshot on any AliExpress page — comparing prices across AliExpress, Amazon, Wildberries, Alibaba, Shein, Naver Shopping, Taobao, and 1688; One-Click Sourcing & Import from any AliExpress page into your DSers Import List; and Coupon-Automated Ordering that applies collected AliExpress coupons at checkout.How does the price comparison feature work?
Simply snap a screenshot on any AliExpress product page, and the extension instantly shows the same product's prices across multiple platforms including AliExpress, Amazon, Wildberries, Alibaba, Shein, Naver Shopping, Taobao, and 1688 — helping you identify the best supplier before placing orders.Is the DSers Chrome Extension free?
Yes, the DSers Chrome Extension is completely free to install and use. It works alongside your DSers account to streamline product sourcing and order management without any additional cost. Coupon collection and some connected DSers features may depend on your plan.Does the extension support coupon savings at checkout?
Yes. The extension automatically applies the AliExpress coupons you have already collected during checkout. You only need to set your preferred currency and payment method once, and the extension handles coupon application automatically. Coupon eligibility still depends on the AliExpress account, order, and DSers plan.How does the extension fit into DSers' overall dropshipping workflow?
The extension serves as the front-end sourcing companion for DSers, which supports sourcing from AliExpress, Alibaba.com, and 1688, and helps sellers manage order processing and tracking sync across connected sales channels. Products imported via the extension flow directly into your DSers Import List for further listing and fulfillment automation.Do I need a DSers account to use the extension?
Yes. Log in to the DSers account that should receive your imports. If prompted, sign in, refresh the page, and try again. Multiple DSers accounts in the same browser session are not supported.What happens after I import a product?
The product appears in your DSers Import List, where you can review and edit its title, description, variants, images, and pricing before publishing. Importing does not publish it automatically.
