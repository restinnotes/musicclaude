Overview
China sourcing assistant. Find 1688 & AliExpress suppliers by image, estimate shipping, and calculate profit margins.
Stop paying retail prices for your inventory. Cut out the middlemen, source directly from China's primary factories, and multiply your profit margins with GP Sourcing.
GP Sourcing is the ultimate browser workspace built for Shopify store owners, D2C brands, and dropshippers looking to scale their business. By bridging competitor spying, factory-direct sourcing, instant DDP shipping estimation, and net profit calculation into a single browser panel, we help you source like a local.
Why E-Commerce Sellers Love GP Sourcing:
1. Reverse Image Search in 1-Click (Bypass Retail Markups)
- Factory Sourcing: Hover over any product image on retail websites or crop a custom screenshot to instantly query 1688 for the original factory listing. Find the exact supplier behind any trending product in seconds.
- Supplier Auditing: Compare factory pricing, transaction history, customer repeat rates, and merchant ratings side-by-side to ensure you only deal with high-quality manufacturers.
2. Landed Cost & DDP Freight Calculator
- No More Waiting: Stop waiting 24–48 hours for shipping agents to quote. Input your product weight and dimensions to get real-time DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping estimates directly inside your sidebar.
- Parcel Customization: Auto-filter by cargo types (Standard, Battery, Liquid, or Sensitive) to get precise quotes and prevent customs clearance issues.
3. Live Currency Conversion & Margin Estimator
- True Margin Math: Automatically convert wholesale RMB prices to USD, EUR, GBP, or JPY using live exchange rates.
- Profit Projection: Input your retail price, and the built-in BI engine will calculate your net profit, markup multiplier, and break-even point in real-time.
4. Shortlist Manager & 1-Click Export
- Build Your Collection: Save potential winners to your local shortlist as you research competitor stores.
- Direct Export: Download clean CSV files formatted for your ERP system, or generate pre-formatted WhatsApp inquiries with image previews to share directly with your sourcing agents.
5. 100% Secure, Private & Ready to Go
- Zero Configuration: No complex API keys or setup required. Install and start sourcing immediately.
- Enterprise-Grade Privacy: Your sourcing data is your most valuable asset. GP Sourcing runs in a local sandbox environment. We do not track, store, or sell your product research data, store credentials, or personal logs.
