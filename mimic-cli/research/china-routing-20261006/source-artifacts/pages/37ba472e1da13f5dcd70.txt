SUPERBUY STORY
WHO
Superbuy founded in 2012, builds the most prestigious platform for overseas users to shop online Chinese goods with ease.
Superbuy is an Internet-based platform where global users can enjoy easy access to one-stop services ranging from purchasing, order fulfillment, quality inspection, international shipping (through third-party couriers) to after-sales. Currently, users can shop from over 20 well-known Chinese e-commerce platforms on Superbuy, and a variety of quality service providers have partnered with Superbuy to help all users to shop from China easier than ever before!
WHAT
Quality shopping agent, warehousing, quality inspection, and parcel forwarding services that will solve all your problems when purchasing Chinese products online.
Helps users abroad purchase Chinese products with peace of mind.
WHY
Professional shopping agent service with free standard services. We will deliver Chinese products to you no matter where you are.
9/7 (09:00-18:00, BJ Time) Online Bilingual Customer Service and CEO Direct Email to help you shop from China worry-free!
HOW
When you want to purchase Chinese products from a foreign country, you just need to log into the Superbuy website or download the APP. Just paste the link of the product or enter the name of the product in the search bar, you will be able to purchase the product you want. After the product arrives at Superbuy’s warehouse, submit the parcel and the parcel will be forwarded to you.
