U.S. Sales Data
U.S. market size for the total toy industry for 2025, projected to 100% of the market, was approximately $45.6 billion, according to Circana Checkout.*
U.S. retail sales of toys generated $30.3 billion in 2025 and grew by +6% vs. last year according to Circana’s U.S. Retail Tracking Services that covers 68% of the U.S. Toys Market.**   While retail toy sales grew 6% from 2024 to 2025, the total dollar value of the industry has remained relatively flat compared with 2021.***
U.S. toy industry dollar volume and trends by Super Category shows that Games/Puzzles has emerged as the largest toy category, generating $4.9 billion in 2025.****
