U.S. consumer network attached storage market highlights
The U.S. consumer network attached storage market generated a revenue of USD 1,704.5 million in 2025 and is expected to reach USD 4,096.9 million by 2033.
The U.S. market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 11.5% from 2026 to 2033.
In terms of segment, 4-bays was the largest revenue generating design in 2025.
2-Bays is the most lucrative design segment registering the fastest growth during the forecast period.
