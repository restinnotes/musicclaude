Computers and peripherals in the US
Euromonitor report with data and analysis of the US computer and peripheral market.

Apple Inc. is the leading player in 2025, with a retail volume share of 24%.
Retail volume sales are set to fall at a CAGR of 3% over the forecast period to 80 million units.
