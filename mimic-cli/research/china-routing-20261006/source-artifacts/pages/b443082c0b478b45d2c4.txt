The official AliExpress dropshipping solution that automates your product sourcing, ordering, and fulfillment
Note
If you're new to dropshipping or switching from another tool, you've probably heard of DSers but aren't sure exactly what it does—or why you need it.DSers Overview
This article gives you a clear, no‑fluff overview of DSers: what it is, what it can do for your business, and why thousands of dropshippers use it every day.
Click here to start dropshipping now with DSers
What Is DSers?
DSers is the official AliExpress, Alibaba and 1688 Dropshipping partner. Think of it as a bridge between supply platforms (where suppliers list products) and your online store (where customers buy).What is DSers
Without DSers, fulfilling a single order takes 5–10 minutes of manual work: copy customer address → log into AliExpress → select correct variant → paste address → place order → copy tracking number → paste back to your store.
With DSers, that same order takes less than 10 seconds. And you can process 100+ orders at once.
What Can DSers Do for Your Business?
Here are the core features that save you time and help you scale:
1. One-Click Product Import
Browse AliExpress as usual. When you find a product you want to sell, click the DSers Chrome extension button. The product (title, images, variants, description) imports instantly to your DSers dashboard—no manual copying.Import Products from AliExpress by DSers Chrom extension
2. Bulk Order Placement
When customers buy from your store, all orders appear in DSers automatically. You can select up to 100 orders and place them on AliExpress with a single click. DSers auto‑fills the customer's shipping address and matches variants for you.Bulk place order on DSers
3. Automatic Tracking Sync
After the supplier ships the order, DSers grabs the tracking number and automatically updates your store and your customer. No copy‑paste. No manual emails.Auto Tracking Sync
4. Price and Inventory Sync
Suppliers change prices or run out of stock. DSers notifies you (and can automatically update your store prices on paid plans). You'll never sell a product at a loss or oversell out‑of‑stock items.Auto Inventory UpdateAuto Price Update
5. Product Editing & Optimization
Supplier listings often have messy titles and descriptions. DSers lets you edit everything—or use AI tools to generate cleaner titles, better descriptions, and enhanced images before pushing products to your store.Product AI Edit Function
6. Multi-Store Management
Running multiple Shopify or WooCommerce stores? DSers connects to all of them from one account. Manage products and fulfill orders across stores without logging in and out.DSers Partner stores

FAQ Section
Everything you need to know about DSers—the official AliExpress dropshipping partner that automates product sourcing, ordering, and fulfillment.
What is DSers and who is it for?
+
DSers is the official AliExpress dropshipping partner that automates product sourcing, ordering, and fulfillment. It's built for beginners, growing stores, multi-store owners, and former Oberlo users—no technical skills needed.
What should I know before signing up for DSers?
+
You can start with the Free Plan which includes up to 3,000 products, 3 connected stores, and 50 orders per month. DSers supports multiple sales channels including Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, eBay, Amazon, and more. You can upgrade anytime as your business grows.
What happens after I sign up?
+
You'll connect your sales channel (takes less than 3 minutes, no coding required), then you can start importing products from AliExpress using the Chrome extension and fulfilling orders automatically.
How does DSers save me time compared to manual dropshipping?
+
Manual fulfillment takes 5–10 minutes per order—copying addresses, placing orders, pasting tracking numbers. With DSers, the same order takes less than 10 seconds, and you can process 100+ orders at once.
Can I use DSers with multiple stores?
+
Yes. DSers connects to multiple Shopify, WooCommerce, and other stores from one account—manage products and fulfill orders across all stores without logging in and out.
Does DSers support suppliers beyond AliExpress?
+
Yes. DSers supports product sourcing and fulfillment from AliExpress, 1688, Alibaba.com, and Agent, helping sellers manage multi-supplier workflows from a single dashboard.
Is there a free plan?
+
Yes. The Free Plan includes up to 3,000 products, 3 connected stores, 50 orders per month, and 1 user account. Upgrade to paid plans for higher limits, AI features, faster sync speeds, and priority support.
