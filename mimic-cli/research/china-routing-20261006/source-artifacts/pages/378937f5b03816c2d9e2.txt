How long will it take for the merchant to ship the ordered goods?
Domestic Taobao sellers generally ship within 3 to 7 days (except for some customized and pre-sale items)
1688 self-operated logistics will ship on the same day (non-self-operated, franchised merchants ship within 3-5 days)
How long will it take for the shipped goods to be delivered to HandHunt?
The HandHunt warehouse is located in Wuhan, Hubei
For shipments from Central China, it usually takes 1-2 working days to arrive. For shipments from the Pearl River Delta, it usually takes 2-4 working days to arrive. For shipments from the Yangtze River Delta, it usually takes 3-5 working days to arrive. For shipments from other remote areas, it usually takes about 5-10 working days to arrive.
Please note that the above time is only an estimate, and the actual time may vary due to weather, traffic and other factors.
Purchasing Disclaimer
HandHunt is committed to eliminating all your worries about international online shopping. In order to ensure that the goods you purchased can be received smoothly, we will have corresponding processing standards for the goods that have arrived. Since Taobao and other similar websites may have illegal commercial practices, please shop with caution; the following special circumstances are deemed to be your consent and follow-up operations. If you have any doubts, please contact the customer service staff to verify when submitting the order.
1. Sensitive goods:
Containing sensitive items such as essential oils, care solutions, lubricants, batteries, glue, etc., in order to ensure your normal delivery, we will take them out for you.
2. Customs policy:
Containing brands, large amounts of liquids\powders, live goods, food\drugs, etc. may be subject to certain shipping risks due to customs policies. If the consequences arise from the above risks, this site is not responsible for customs clearance and bears relevant legal responsibilities.
3. Unable to professionally inspect goods:
If you purchase electrical appliances, brands, tickets, cards, models and other goods, we will not be able to conduct professional (quality, authenticity, integrity, etc.) inspections for the goods you ordered; when the quality inspector inspects the goods, he can only check whether the appearance of the goods is intact and whether the accessories are complete, but cannot turn on the machine to check whether the goods have quality problems.
4. Fragile items:
Containing ceramics, glass products, irregularly shaped products (wipers, car fenders, etc.), etc., the products may be worn and broken during transportation. Please purchase with caution. We guarantee the integrity of the products before delivery, but if the products are damaged during delivery and are judged as fragile by logistics, the losses incurred shall be borne by you.
5. Customized products:
If the order you submit is a customized product, it is recommended that you negotiate with the merchant. Order the product according to your ideas and creativity. After successful negotiation with the merchant, you need to provide the merchant's Wangwang, contact information or code. We cannot conduct professional inspections for such products for you, and the losses incurred shall be borne by you.
6. Deposit order:
Due to the regulations of Taobao sellers, deposit orders cannot be cancelled after being photographed, and you need to follow up the information released by the seller to make up the balance before delivery. If the balance is not paid within the time limit, the deposit will not be refunded.
7. Low seller reputation:
It is recommended that you choose a store with a store reputation of more than three hearts and pay a Taobao deposit to purchase goods. Stores with low merchant credibility and no insurance premiums may have disputes such as sellers not shipping, false shipping, after-sales, and rights protection. You will be responsible for any losses incurred.
8. "CD" and "hand-made" products:
Since "CD" and "hand-made" products have high collection value, in order not to affect the user's collection, the inspection standard for such products is not to remove the plastic bag for inspection. Only the appearance can be inspected for damage, and the details of the internal parts cannot be specifically verified. The risks incurred shall be borne by you.
If the order you submit has the above risks, we will regard you as having read and accepted the above terms. Please be sure to read them carefully. HandHunt reserves all rights of interpretation!
Parcel Transshipment Inspection Rules
Receipt Inspection Rules:
1. When signing for a parcel, if the outer packaging is found to be severely damaged or other goods that do not meet the delivery requirements, the warehouse staff will directly refuse to accept or return the goods.
2. If your parcel meets the delivery requirements, the warehouse will unpack all parcels for prohibited items after receiving the goods, and then weigh them for storage.
3. If it is found that your goods do not meet the requirements of transportation and air transportation, we will make a special mark on the relevant order to remind you to negotiate the return.
4. If you have not found the warehouse's delivery information within a reasonable domestic delivery time (generally the domestic delivery cycle is 3-5 days), please contact your sender/domestic logistics company or website customer service staff to confirm the status of the goods.
Inspection and warehousing instructions
We will check and inspect the quantity, color, size and other specifications of the goods. After the inspection is completed, the original express box will be removed, and the Pandabuy self-sealing bag will be used to pack the goods and arrange for storage. (Fragile, easy to break, express boxes are used as product packaging boxes, and there are oversized products, etc., and the original boxes are retained. The specific situation is subject to actual operation)
Notes:
a. Color difference, size error, use effect, etc. are not within the scope of inspection;
b. Electrical appliances, digital products, peripheral products and other highly professional products, their quality, model, spare parts, etc. are not within the scope of inspection;
c. "CD" and "hand-made" and "adult products" products, such sealed packaging and with sealing labels or sealing strips are inspected without unpacking. Only the appearance can be inspected for damage, and the details of the internal parts or functional problems cannot be specifically verified;

International shipping costs: the shipping costs calculated based on the weight of your goods, the selected shipping method, and the shipping area
Additional services:

HandHunt's purchasing processSelecting Products
Method 1: Copying Product Links
Users can first browse on Chinese shopping platforms (Taobao, Tmall, 1688, etc.), copy the link of the desired product, and paste it into the search box on the HandHunt page. The platform will automatically capture the product information.
Method 2: Searching for Products
Users can search for products by name on the HandHunt platform and select from the results on Taobao, Tmall, and 1688.com.
Tips
Currently, the platform is applicable to Taobao, Tmall, and 1688.
Can't find the item? Try giving feedback to the customer service.
Placing Orders
Users can select the color, size, and quantity of the products. If there is any additional information they wish to convey to the seller (such as merchant discounts or seller contact information), they can leave a Tips in the remark section. To place an order immediately, users can select "Buy Now"; if they wish to browse more products and place orders later, they can select "Add to Cart."
Tips
Please note that due to certain items being restricted for international shipping, there will be risk reminders.
Most products on 1688 have a minimum order quantity. Please refer to the supplier's minimum quantity order.
Confirming Orders
If the user selects "Buy Now" in the previous step or clicks "Checkout" after selecting products in the "Cart" page, they will be redirected to this page where they can reconfirm the order details and select the final delivery country.
Paying for Orders
After submitting the order, users will be redirected to the checkout page where they can choose a payment method to complete the payment. Payment options include PayPal, credit cards, WeChat Pay, Alipay, Chinese debit cards, and bank wire transfers.
Tips
● Supports multiple currencies
● Payment methods: PayPal, credit card, WeChat Pay, Alipay, Chinese debit card, bank wire transfer.
HandHunt Platform Purchase
HandHunt will contact the seller and purchase the ordered items on the user's behalf.
Tips
During the purchase process, you can contact our customer service for any further details. We will contact you via email or message if needed.
Inspection and Storage
Quality inspections will be conducted, and the items will be stored in the warehouse. Users can verify the inspection information by selecting "Inspected" under the "My Orders" section and clicking "View Inspection Photos."
Tips:
After the items arrive at our warehouse, they will undergo a series of quality checks, and photos will be taken for your reference. If you have any questions within 24 hours after we confirm their correctness, please contact us immediately.
Products purchased from 1688 have a different set of quality control standards.
Submitting Orders from the Warehouse
From the "My Warehouse" section, select "In Stock," check the items, fill in the shipping details, and submit the order.
HandHunt offers additional services such as package removal, reinforcement, and insurance. Please inform us of any such requirements.
Tips:
We offer additional services such as package removal, package reinforcement, insurance, etc. Please let us know and we will follow your requirements.
Paying for International Shipping
Select "Pending Payment" from the "My Warehouse" section to pay for the international shipping fees.
Tips
The international shipping deposit is calculated based on the estimated weight of the product, the selected shipping method, and the destination. The final shipping fee will be based on the package's actual size and weight verified by the shipping company. Any difference between the final fee and the deposit will be refunded to the user's payment account after the product is shipped.
Awaiting Shipment
In the "Awaiting Shipment" section of "My Warehouse," items will be consolidated, packaged, reinforced, weighed, and shipped. An international tracking number will be generated accordingly.
Tips
There may be cases of domestic returns due to customs issues such as additional taxes. In such cases, users may be required to pay the additional taxes. If the package is returned to the warehouse due to non-payment of taxes or for safety reasons, and the user still wishes to resend it, they need to handle the matter accordingly in the "My Package" section.
Shipped
You can check the international logistics status and track the shipping progress of your package under the "Shipped" section in "My Warehouse."
Tips
During international package transportation, accidents can always occur. Such situations include delays, confiscation, taxes, package damage, package loss, etc. The HandHunt platform cannot control or prevent accidents that may occur when your package is in the hands of third-party logistics companies or customs. We also cannot guarantee the service quality of other companies unrelated to the HandHunt platform. However, our goal is to provide the best service to our customers, and we will notify you ahead of time about the shipping conditions for each route or country you choose, and do our best to assist you when problems arise.
If the package is returned to the warehouse due to reasons such as failed delivery, you need to split the package in "My Packages" if you still want to resend it.
Delivered
The package has been successfully delivered to you. If you have any questions about the package, you can select the relevant package and add after-sales service Tipss.
Tips
We determine whether a product has been delivered based on the logistics delivery to your hands. If you have any disputes with the order, please feel free to contact our customer service team, and we will do our best to assist you.
