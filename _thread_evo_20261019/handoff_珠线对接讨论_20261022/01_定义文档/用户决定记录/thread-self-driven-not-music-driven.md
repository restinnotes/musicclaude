User (2026-10-21), after I said B0 is vivid partly because of "精确到小节的音乐事件":

- Precise bar-level music events have nothing to do with being vivid (鲜活). Don't call it that.
- Judges so far never penalized the "discrete" (离散型) pattern: each segment opens with "此时音乐忽然……" and the music event drives the change.
- The Thread must read on its own as one logical, time-ordered process. Each turn comes from what happened before inside the person. It cannot be "now suddenly a music event happens" with the music driving the feeling.

**Why:** the line is the backbone. If music events supply the causes, the text is a commentary on the score, and the why-now logic is outsourced to the music.

**How to apply:**
- Judges check each segment's driver: internal (earlier experience or understanding) vs music event vs none. Music-driven or discrete segments pull the overall score down.
- Writing prompts: music may appear only as the outward shape or evidence of a state that is already motivated, never as the reason for it.
- Do not reward score-detail precision as "alive".
