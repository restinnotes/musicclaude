Overview
Official 1688 Plugin for screenshots, product searches, sales trends, downloads, and Wangwang notifications.
The 1688 Procurement Assistant Plugin is a free extension developed by the official 1688 platform, aimed at helping users improve their e-commerce purchasing and operational efficiency. It supports one-click screenshot search for similar products across the web, sales and price trend queries, downloading of product materials from 1688, notifications for Wangwang messages and order updates, AI-powered keyword splitting and combination, downloading and analysis of reviews, exporting of entire store products, and more. We hope it becomes your trusted business partner.
🥳 Current plugin features include:
1. One-Click Screenshot Search for Similar Products – Take screenshots on any website to search for similar products via image, such as 1688, Taobao, Douyin Shop, Amazon, OZON, JD.com, Pinduoduo, etc. Multiple convenient screenshot methods are supported, including right-click with mouse or using keyboard shortcuts.
2. Multi-Platform Price Comparison – Image-based price comparison supports 1688, Taobao, Amazon, AliExpress, Alibaba, and will continue to expand to more platforms, making it easy to compare prices across platforms and find the best sourcing options.
3. Image Search Result Filtering & Sorting – Image search results support various filtering conditions, such as “Show Factories Only”, “Supports Sample Orders”, “Free Shipping”, “Private Delivery”, etc. You can also sort results based on your needs by price, sales volume, fulfillment quantity, and more, to efficiently locate the desired 1688 products.
4. Export Product Images & Materials – Batch download images and video materials for comparing similar products. Customize filtering of 1688 product information and export to Excel files, facilitating procurement and inventory operations.
5. View Sales / Price Trends – On 1688 product detail pages, view data such as listing date, update date, transaction trends, price trends, and quantity vs. price relationships. Help with business decisions and insights, powered directly by official 1688 data—efficient, accurate, and real.
6. Distribute Products – Distribution users can switch to distribution mode from image search results, view more 1688 product distribution data and information, select multiple items, and distribute with one click.
7. Wangwang & Order Notifications – Receive push notifications for 1688 Wangwang messages and logistics/order updates to avoid missing business opportunities.
8. Search for Suppliers Across the Web – By searching product titles across the web, quickly find similar products for comprehensive comparisons, making it easier to choose the most cost-effective suppliers and maximize procurement efficiency.
9. Keyword Splitting & Combination – On 1688 product detail pages, easily split and combine any title into new product titles. Our AI assistant will help you select the best titles to increase exposure in search engines and boost product visibility and appeal!
10. Freight Cost Inquiry – Easily check shipping costs directly on 1688 product detail pages to assist in accurate cost estimation and help develop more reasonable procurement strategies.
11. Review Analysis – On 1688 product detail pages, gain deep insights from user feedback through review analysis. The AI system summarizes comment content and provides multi-dimensional evaluations to help identify issues and opportunities.
12. Export Entire Store Products – One-click export of all product information within a 1688 store, enabling centralized management and follow-up data analysis to improve operational efficiency.
13. Order Logistics Tracking – On the 1688 order list page, directly view detailed logistics status for all orders, allowing efficient order management and improved operational performance.
👉 We are continuously improving the official 1688 Procurement Assistant Plugin to meet the needs of professional buyers, including AI-powered product comparison, AI-driven business opportunity analytics, cross-border procurement, and more.
