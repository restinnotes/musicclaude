Overview
Find 1688 suppliers from Amazon pages with image search, AI translation, supplier analysis, and fast product matching.
Amazon to 1688 Sourcing Assistant helps Amazon sellers source products faster by connecting Amazon product pages with 1688 supplier discovery tools.
Use the extension to find matching products on 1688, review suppliers, translate product and supplier pages into English, and speed up sourcing research without leaving your workflow.
Key features:
- Amazon to 1688 image-based product search
- AI supplier analysis for faster evaluation
- English translation for 1688 product and supplier pages
- Search-result scoring to review supplier quality faster
- Product matching directly from Amazon pages
- Order export and sourcing workflow support
Built for Amazon sellers, online arbitrage teams, private label researchers, sourcing assistants, and e-commerce operators who want a faster way to move from product discovery to supplier validation.
If you source from China for the US market, this extension makes Amazon to 1688 research faster, clearer, and easier to scale.
