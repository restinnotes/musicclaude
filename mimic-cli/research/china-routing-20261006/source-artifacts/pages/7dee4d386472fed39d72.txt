U.S. power tools market highlights
The U.S. power tools market generated a revenue of USD 7,541.4 million in 2025 and is expected to reach USD 15,043.4 million by 2033.
The U.S. market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 9.3% from 2026 to 2033.
In terms of segment, drills was the largest revenue generating product in 2025.
Drills is the most lucrative product segment registering the fastest growth during the forecast period.
