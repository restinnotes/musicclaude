Capital One Shopping: A free tool to find deals online
March 12, 2026|3 min read
Saving money while shopping online is always better when it’s easy. And Capital One Shopping makes finding great deals online a breeze. It instantly finds and tests coupon codes, searches retailers for better prices and notifies you when prices drop. It even lets you earn rewards to redeem for gift cards.
Plus, Capital One Shopping is free.
What you’ll learn:
Capital One Shopping makes it easier to save money while shopping online.
Capital One Shopping works with most major internet browsers, and it’s free.
You can also use the Capital One Shopping app on most phones.
Capital One Shopping automatically finds and tests coupon and promo codes, gives you instant price comparisons, tells you if prices drop on items you’ve viewed and lets you earn rewards.

What is Capital One Shopping?
Capital One Shopping is a free browser extension and mobile app that automatically searches for online coupons, better prices and rewards at over 100,000 online retailers. That makes it easier to save money when you shop online.
Is there a fee for Capital One Shopping?
There are no fees for using Capital One Shopping. It’s free for everyone, even people who aren’t already Capital One customers.
How does Capital One Shopping work?
Using the Capital One Shopping extension is simple. It works with all major browsers, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge and Safari. You can also use the Capital One Shopping app on iOS and Android devices.
Once you install the browser extension or app, you can shop normally. Capital One Shopping works quietly in the background to help you save money. Here’s how.
Coupon codes
When it’s time to check out, Capital One Shopping searches for available coupons and promo codes, automatically applying valid ones to your purchase. That can save you money and a lot of time looking for codes that may not work.
Price comparisons
Capital One Shopping automatically compares prices online when you shop at certain retailers. It even checks shipping costs and membership pricing.
When it finds a better price, Capital One Shopping shows you the cost difference and provides a link to the retailer with the better deal.
Price-drop notifications
Capital One Shopping keeps track of items you’ve viewed and may send you a notification when a price drops.
Rewards
Even if discounts or better prices aren’t available, you could still earn rewards by making eligible purchases through Capital One Shopping partner sites. These rewards are independent of the rewards you might earn with a Capital One rewards credit card.
How to redeem Capital One Shopping Rewards
You can redeem your Capital One Shopping Rewards for digital gift cards from dozens of retailers. Once you’ve redeemed your rewards, it can take up to three business days for gift cards to appear in your account.
Do Capital One Shopping Rewards expire?
Capital One Shopping Rewards won’t expire as long as your account is active and in good standing.
Key takeaways: Capital One Shopping
Saving money with Capital One Shopping is simple. Once you’re signed up, it starts working immediately, searching for coupons and better prices when you shop online. There’s nothing you need to do except shop, sit back and enjoy the savings.
