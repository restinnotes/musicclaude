2026 U.S. Consumer Technology Ownership & Market Potential Study
Published on May 2026
Accessibility
Audio and Video
Digital Health
Smart Home
This CTA Research study is conducted annually across ten consumer technology categories spanning 73 technology products among U.S. households, with a goal of establishing two key metrics:
Degree of Ownership – Establish household penetration (ownership) rates of consumer technology products within the U.S.
Purchase Intent – Monitor the proportion of U.S. households planning to purchase consumer technology products within the next 12 months
The study also identifies initial household penetrations of emerging tech products.
Technologies covered include:
TVs (e.g., OLED, 4K, smart TVs)
Smartwatches
Video game consoles
Computing devices (e.g., laptops, desktops, tablets)
Smartphones
Smart home devices
Audio devices
Lifestyle tech (e.g., pet tech, drones, electric scooters)
And many more
