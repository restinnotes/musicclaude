Overview
AiPrice Search by Image allows you to easily find the source goods on e-commerce websites.
Main functions:
1. Search for the same style with pictures
- Supports searching for the same or similar products through images on Amazon, AliExpress, Alibaba International Station, 1688 and other platforms. For more platforms, please check out the AiPrice official website: https://www.aiprice.com
- Image search method: click the image search icon on the product image, right-click the image, screenshot search, or upload a local image
- Picture enlargement: On the product list page, move the mouse over the picture to quickly preview the large picture.
2. Utility toolbar
- Download pictures and videos: Download product pictures and videos in batches from Alibaba, AliExpress, Amazon, Taobao and other platforms
- Export image link: Copy or export product image URL to facilitate supply organization, shelf preparation and team collaboration
- Copy product link: Copy the product link of supported platforms with one click
- Copy product title: Copy the product title and use the translated title to search for similar products
- Freight Inquiry: Quickly check the estimated shipping costs in different regions
- Export store products: Export the store product list to an Excel file
- SKU viewing and price calculation: View SKU details and calculate product costs more efficiently
- eBay sales records: Jump to the purchase record page with one click to quickly check the product transaction price, purchase quantity and transaction time
- AI product drawing: supports batch product refinement, copying of competing product images, image translation, high-definition enlargement, intelligent cutout and product package processing, and rapid production of shelves, advertisements, main images, detail images and social media materials.
3. Price history and product research
- View product price history on Amazon, AliExpress and other supported platforms. Supports viewing long-term price and order trends, identifying true discounts, and avoiding misleading promotions
- Add products to your favorites and monitor prices. When prices change, the system will automatically send notification reminders
- Product Information Pro: Display additional product details such as shipping cost, order price, minimum order quantity, annual sales volume, shelf time, store information, etc. on supported platforms
- Advanced query: compare similar products, filter results, sort product data, and export to Excel
4. Amazon Product Pro
- Display key data such as category ranking, BSR, ASIN, number of Amazon self-operated, and FBA sellers on the Amazon search results page
- Supports quick viewing of product trends and information to help sellers determine competition, sales performance and product selection value
5. Translation Tools
- Web page translation: right-click to translate the entire web page
- Screenshot translation: capture part of the page and translate the text in the screenshot
- Image translation: translate text in product images and generate new images
- Chat Translation: Translate chats with sellers on supported platforms
6. Comment related
- Review analysis: Analyze reviewer country distribution on supported product pages
- Download reviews: Download product reviews and review images from Amazon, AliExpress and other platforms
7. Exchange rate conversion
- Convert product prices to U.S. dollars, Chinese yuan, Korean won and other currencies
- Display RMB prices on supported international shopping websites
8. Logistics monitoring
- View package logistics information directly on the supported order page
9. Copy associated words
- Copy keywords and related search terms recommended under the search boxes of websites such as Amazon
10. Export shopping cart
- Export the products in the shopping cart of the supported platform to Excel files to facilitate purchasing
11. Browsing history
- View recent browsing history of supported e-commerce platforms through the plug-in toolbar
12. Highlight advertising products
- Highlight advertising labels to help users differentiate between organic results and promoted products
13. Plug-in minimization
- Expand the plug-in window in the corner of the web page
Applicable people:
- E-commerce sellers
- No-source model (Dropshipping) sellers
- Product selection staff
- Supply purchasing team
- Store operations staff
- Supply chain team
- Designers and product listing optimization team
