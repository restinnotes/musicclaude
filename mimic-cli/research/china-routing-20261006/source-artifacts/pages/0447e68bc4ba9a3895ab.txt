AiPrice (formerly AliPrice) Sourcing Tool
Home/Others/AiPrice (formerly AliPrice) Sourcing Tool
AiPrice (formerly AiPrice) Sourcing Tool belongs to Dongguan Chengpai Network Technology Co., Ltd. It is a one-click image search and product sourcing tool specially designed for cross-border e-commerce sellers.
1. Introduction to AiPrice
AiPrice is a company focused on the development and operation of cross-border e-commerce SaaS software.
As of August 2025, AiPrice has over 1 million total users, with nearly 650,000 registered users. Its website ranks about 20,000 globally on Alexa, and around 1,000 in South Korea. Over 70% of local merchants in South Korea choose the AiPrice extension, demonstrating its strong influence and popularity in the international market.
2. Core Features
Image Search for Same Products across multiple e-commerce platforms including 1688, Taobao, Alibaba, Amazon, AliExpress, Shopee, SHEIN, Ozon, Naver Shopping, eBay, Pinduoduo, Google Lens, Soukuan, Yandex, Wildberries, Domeggook, Coupang, Netsea, Ownerclan, Onch3, Mercari, etc.
Product Price History Tracking.
Product Collection and Export Functions.
One-stop Product Sourcing and Operation Services, helping cross-border sellers improve sourcing and listing efficiency.
3. Use Cases
Image Search: One-click search for identical products, helping users find cheaper sources across multiple platforms (such as 1688, Taobao, Alibaba, AliExpress, etc.), discover untapped market opportunities, and create potential bestsellers.
Price Comparison: Quickly compare the same product’s prices across different stores/platforms to find the best procurement channel; also calculate profit margins based on market prices to support pricing strategies.
Price History Tracking: View 3-month, 6-month, or 1-year historical price records to identify real discounts; track product prices and receive notifications when prices drop/rise, with email alerts supported.
Product Collection and Export: Supports bulk export of product information (such as titles, images, links, prices, etc.); capture real-time product details and sales data from the current page for easier listing or comparative analysis.
4. Target Users
Cross-border sellers: Search for identical products to find suppliers, compare prices, and optimize listings.
Agents/Buyers: Monitor price trends to identify the lowest prices and high-quality supply channels.
End consumers: Compare prices, check historical prices, and avoid overpaying.
