The Superbuy Affiliate Program is a long-term incentive plan open to users worldwide. By sharing your unique invite link or invite code, you can earn bonus rewards from every international parcel shipping fee paid by friends you refer — with no upper limit.
QHow is my bonus calculated?
Calculation method:
Your Current Affiliate Bonus Rate - Your Friend's Member Shipping Discount Rate = Actual Bonus Rate × Your Friend's Actual Shipping Fee
Note: If the Actual Bonus Rate < 0, your actual applicable bonus rate will be 1%.
Bonus Settlement & Withdrawal:
Bonuses from the previous month (excluding frozen amounts) will be issued uniformly on the 1st of each month. You may request withdrawal at any time. Superbuy will complete the transfer within 10 business days (delayed in case of Chinese New Year holidays).
