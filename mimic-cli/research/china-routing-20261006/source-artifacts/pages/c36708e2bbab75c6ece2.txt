U.S. wearable technology market highlights
The U.S. wearable technology market generated a revenue of USD 25,165.5 million in 2025 and is expected to reach USD 56,454.7 million by 2033.
The U.S. market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 10.8% from 2026 to 2033.
In terms of segment, wrist-wear was the largest revenue generating product in 2025.
Wrist-Wear is the most lucrative product segment registering the fastest growth during the forecast period.
