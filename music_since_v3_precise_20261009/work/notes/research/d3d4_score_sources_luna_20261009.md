# D3/D4 score/source research — Luna — 2026-10-09

## Scope and handling

Independent Step A source review for Chopin, Barcarolle in F-sharp major, Op. 60, mm. 16–35 (priority, especially mm. 24–27); Ballade No. 4 in F minor, Op. 52, mm. 191–203; and Polonaise-Fantaisie, Op. 61, m. 24. This note records score-visible or source-authored musical facts only: phrase, voice, texture, rhythm, register, dynamics, and harmonic direction. It makes no affect judgment, no Step B/C decision, and no segmentation or boundary decision. In particular, m. 24 is not proposed as a cut, and mm. 24–27 are not split into multiple cuts.

No audio was opened or listened to. Every audio claim is therefore **audio-unverified**. H4/H5/Center were not consulted. The main terminal gate and real-music hold were not examined or cleared by this research.

The prior F-web / run53 summaries are treated as conflicting claims to check, never as evidence. A failure to find a phrase in an author’s prose is not proof that the phrase is false; it is reported as unsupported here unless the score itself was read for that precise fact. Run53’s “recognition” and affect narration are not score facts and are not assessed; its objective ornament-density candidate is checked only against the cited score/source evidence.

## Evidence labels

- **web-verified** — the cited source page or its indexed text was opened and checked.
- **score-example-verified** — an author-published score example and its stated measure span were checked. This does not imply a fresh transcription of every note.
- **full-score-verified** — a public facsimile page was visually inspected for the stated notation, or its exact bar/page locator is verified where that narrower limitation is stated.
- **audio-unverified** — no listening-based judgment was made.

For original editions, the bar numbers below are continuous measure numbers assigned by Chopin’s First Editions Online (CFEO) to facsimile pages; the nineteenth-century printed pages themselves do not print continuous bar numbers. This page map is consistent with the conventional numbering used by Kopp and PTNA.

## Findings

### Op. 60 — priority result

| Claim under review | Finding | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| mm. 20–22 A-sharp pedal; return to F-sharp at m. 24 | Kopp describes an extended A-sharp pedal in mm. 20–22, heard as V/vi in context, followed by resolution from an A-sharp major triad directly to F-sharp at m. 24. His account is **web-verified** (paras. 16–17). The 1846 French First Edition pages are p. 2 (mm. 14–23) and p. 3 (mm. 24–33); both were inspected. The harmonic wording is Kopp’s analysis, not a fresh reduction made here. |
| “Main melody temporarily retreats” in mm. 16–23 | Not established as a literal disappearance or rest of the main melody by Kopp’s text. Paragraph 16 describes the harmonic/pedal route but does not state that the melody temporarily withdraws. PTNA’s secondary structure table labels mm. 17–23 an Interlude; that section label is not independent proof of literal melody absence. The primary p. 2 page was inspected, but no bar-by-bar absence claim is made. Treat “retreat” as **unverified**, not as a settled score fact. |
| Theme return and texture at/after m. 24 | Kopp states that theme A / Rink A′ returns at m. 24 in a heightened setting with double trills, melodic diminution, and textural amplification, and describes the later cadence as squarely in the tonic. This is **web-verified** in para. 17. Kopp’s Example 5, p. 4 of the examples PDF, is labelled as theme A′, mm. 23–35; example locator/span are **score-example-verified**. It credits the commercial Henle Urtext edition (E. Herttrich, 1978), so it is not treated as a public-domain source and was not saved as an asset. CFEO first-edition p. 3 (mm. 24–33) and p. 4 (mm. 34–45) were also inspected. |
| mm. 24–27 as several distinct events or cut points | No. The sources support a return beginning at m. 24 and a longer theme span in Example 5 (mm. 23–35). They do not establish separate affective or segmentation boundaries at m. 24, 25, 26, or 27. No such boundaries are proposed. |
| m. 34 marking | The 1846 French First Edition facsimile, p. 4 (CFEO bars 34–45), visibly prints “rallent.” in the system containing m. 34. This marking is **full-score-verified**. PTNA’s secondary practice note specifies that “From the second beat of measure 34, ‘rall.’ is indicated”; this exact beat placement is **web-verified** only as PTNA commentary. Kopp does not mention a rit./rall. marking. The primary score’s marking is “rallent.”; this review found no basis for rewriting it as “rit.” |
| m. 35 A-sharp 4 to A-natural | Kopp explicitly says the repeated inner-voice A-sharp 4 is transformed to A-natural on its fourth appearance at m. 35 (para. 17): **web-verified**. CFEO p. 4 is the inspected first-edition page containing mm. 34–45, with the m. 35 notation visible. No affect or boundary is inferred from the chromatic change. |

### Op. 52 — mm. 191–203

CFEO identifies impression **52–1-Sm** as the French First Edition. Its page map places mm. 185–202 on p. 11 and mm. 203–222 on p. 12. The p. 11 image was visually inspected; p. 12 did not render legibly in the available viewer. Thus the primary source and exact locator for m. 203 are verified, but its notes were **not independently read** in this pass.

At page level, inspected p. 11 shows recurring chord attacks in the upper staff over a running left-hand sixteenth-note arpeggiation, with more continuous, mobile figuration later on the page. This is a cautious **full-score-verified texture candidate for the inspected page**, not a claim that a particular event occurs at a particular bar among mm. 191–202. No note-level claim is made for m. 203 or the remainder of the requested span. No boundary or affect interpretation is made.

### Op. 61 — m. 24

CFEO identifies impression **61–1-BR** as the French First Edition. The University of Chicago Library facsimile page p. 2 is indexed as mm. 20–43, so it contains m. 24. The primary score page and locator are **web-verified**, but its image rendered blank / unsupported in the available viewer; notation at m. 24 is consequently **not full-score-verified** here. PTNA’s secondary structure table places the beginning of section A at m. 24, but that is not a substitute for reading the primary notation. This item remains a note-level evidence gap.
