Superbuy Fee Structure
The total cost of shopping at Superbuy is transparent and consists of Product Cost, International Shipping Fee, and Value-Added Service Fee. The first two correspond to the two main stages: Stage 1 (Shopping Agent) and Stage 2 (International Shipping). The Value-Added Service Fee is optional based on your personalized needs.
Stage 1: Purchasing Service Fee
(Charged when we buy the item for you domestically.)
This stage covers all costs incurred to ship the item from the Chinese merchant to the Superbuy warehouse. It consists of three components:
Original Product Price
The listed price of the item on platforms such as Taobao or JD.com. Superbuy charges no extra fees on this price.
China Domestic Shipping Fee
The courier fee for shipping from the merchant to the Superbuy warehouse. Superbuy pays this on your behalf without any extra charges (fees vary by merchant; some offer "Free Shipping").
Purchasing Service Fee
This charge represents Superbuy's percentage-based purchasing service fee, calculated on the item cost and determined by its source platform：
Standard Purchasing Service (Free)
No service fee is charged for mainstream platforms such as Taobao, Tmall, JD.com, and 1688.
Secondhand Item Purchasing Service
Xianyu, Paipai, Zhuanzhuan, etc.: Flat fee of CN¥ 20/DI.
Kongfz and other second-hand platforms: 10% service fee based on item value (Min CN¥ 10, Max CN¥ 20).
Note: Rates differ slightly due to the complex communication and after-sales processes involved.
Other Purchasing Services
For unlisted platforms, a 10% service fee based on item value (Min CN¥ 10, Max CN¥ 20).
Shipping Expert
For special items (e.g., pre-sales, limited editions), the "Shipping Expert" service applies with a 10% service fee based on item value (Min CN¥ 20, Max CN¥ 300).
Fee Calculation Example:To buy a piece of clothing for CN¥ 100 on Taobao with CN¥ 10 domestic shipping and Detailed Photos (CN¥ 3.00):
Total Stage 1 Cost: CN¥ 100 (Price) + CN¥ 10 (Shipping) + CN¥ 0 (Service Fee) + CN¥ 3.00 (Photos) = CN¥ 113.00
Reminder (Storage Policy):Once the fees for this stage are paid, the item will be sent to the Superbuy warehouse for storage. Items enjoy a 90-day free storage period upon arrival. Items exceeding the free period will be charged a storage fee of CN¥ 0.1 per item per day. The maximum storage period is 180 days. If no parcel shipping order is submitted or no feedback is provided after 180 days, the item will be considered abandoned and disposed of. To extend the storage, please purchase the "Extend Storage Period" service in advance. For more details, please visit the Help Center.
Stage 2: International Shipping Fee
(Charged when we ship your parcel overseas to you.)
This section covers the fees for the second stage. Once items arrive at the warehouse, you need to pay to ship the parcel to your address. This fee is a comprehensive calculation generated by the system based on the final weight of your parcel, the specific shipping line selected, and the destination country/region. If you have purchased multiple items, Superbuy can consolidate them into one parcel for free, which may potentially lower your international shipping costs.
This fee mainly consists of the following components:
Initial Weight Shipping Fee
The starting price for the shipment weight, corresponding to the cost of the first unit of chargeable weight (e.g., 500g)
+
Additional Weight Shipping Fee
The extra fee charged for each additional unit of weight after the parcel exceeds the first weight
+
Customs Clearance Fee
Import taxes (e.g., tariffs, VAT) levied by the destination country's customs based on item category and declared value when the parcel enters the destination country. This fee is borne by the recipient
+
Fuel Surcharge
An additional percentage fee charged on top of the base freight to cope with oil price fluctuations. Most air freight and courier lines charge this fee to hedge against fluctuations in international fuel prices
+
Operation Fee
A base service fee charged by the warehouse for processing your parcel (including storage, sorting, packing, etc.)
Fee Calculation Example:Suppose you choose the "Hong Kong UPS Economy (Tax-Free)" line to ship a 1000g parcel: International Shipping Fee (CN¥ 428) = CN¥ 320 (First Weight 500g) + CN¥ 50 (Continued Weight 500g) + CN¥ 38 (Customs Fee) .

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Fees & Pricing
Is Superbuy's pricing transparent? Are there hidden fees?
Superbuy's pricing is completely transparent; all fees will be clearly displayed before you place an order. There are no hidden fees, so you can use our services with confidence.
Q: How are your service fees charged, and is basic procurement free of charge?
The shopping agent service fee is charged based on the product's source platform. Ordinary shopping agent service does not charge a service fee.
Are value-added services mandatory? What value-added services are available?
Value-added services are completely optional; you can choose them according to your needs. We offer various value-added services, including inspection, insurance, reinforced packaging, etc.
How do I check my order fee details?
You can view the complete fee breakdown on the order details page, including product price, service fees, shipping fees, and all other fee items.
When is the international shipping fee charged?
The international shipping fee is charged upfront when you submit your shipping order. The system will calculate and collect the payment based on the estimated weight of your parcel, your selected shipping route, and the destination address.Please note that the "estimated weight" is for preliminary calculation only. The final shipping cost will be determined by the logistics provider's official bill.
