User (2026-10-23), after I asked them to rank four drafts on 1–39 and judged the drafts on how well they explain the beads:

> 我觉得我们在这一步的目的是为了让他能有总览的宏观视野 不能让他被bead困住 但是我们必须要有一个时刻让他能结合进bead的 如果说你给我的这个只是中间产物 是不是不该让我看呢

**Why:** each stage has its own goal.
- Drafting (no beads) supplies the whole-piece understanding: Center, history, connections and insight. Scoring drafts on bead explanation measures the wrong thing.
- Explaining the beads is the job of the later integration step, the sectional synthesis that sees the beads.
- The user's reading time should go to products whose quality they actually decide on.

**How to apply:**
- Judge drafts on macro qualities: a convincing whole-piece view, real history and connection, insight, no invented back-and-forth, plain language. Don't judge them on per-segment bead fit.
- Judge bead fit only on the outputs of the integration step.
- Show the user final or near-final outputs. Show an intermediate only when one specific decision needs their eye, and say why.
